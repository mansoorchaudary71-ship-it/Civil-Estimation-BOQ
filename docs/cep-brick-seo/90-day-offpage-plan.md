# 90-Day Off-Page SEO Plan — Brick Calculator (Civil Estimation Pro)

Goal: earn the first quality referring domains for /brick-calculator and its
city/type/size variants, in Pakistan, India and the UAE. No paid links, no link
schemes, no bought directories. Everything below is free unless marked otherwise.

## Week 0 — Foundations (before any outreach)
- [ ] Fix the domain (see CHANGES file): civilestimationpro.com must resolve and
  serve the site over HTTPS, with the CNAME in place. Nothing else works until then.
- [ ] Google Search Console: add the property, submit sitemap.xml, request indexing
  for /brick-calculator + the 16 programmatic URLs. Set the geo-targeting by page
  (there is no single-country target — the site serves PK/IN/AE).
- [ ] Bing Webmaster Tools: same sitemap submission (Bing powers DuckDuckGo and
  matters for the UAE market).
- [ ] Google Business Profile: create a profile for Civil Estimation Pro (service-area
  business, no storefront needed) — it feeds the local-business signals the
  competitors show (Avenir's offices, Muzdoor's registration numbers).
- [ ] Analytics: confirm GA4 (or the existing analytics) records /brick-calculator
  page views and PDF downloads as events.
- [ ] Check the five competitors' backlinks with the free checkers (no account):
  MrKeyword free backlink checker (20 strongest referring domains) and Outrank.so
  free checker. Record the actual referring domains for tameer.ai, visionbuild-
  construction.com.pk, avenirdevelopments.com, muzdoor.com and marshmoorbricks.com.
  Those lists are the replication targets — do not guess them.

## Days 1–30 — Directories, profiles & citations
- [ ] Submit the site to free, relevant directories (construction/engineering only):
  - Pakistan: Pakistan Construction Directory listings, Chamber of Commerce member
    directories (Lahore/Islamabad/Karachi), PakBiz-type business listings.
  - India: IndiaMART company profile (free), JustDial business listing, construction
    trade directories.
  - UAE: Yellow Pages UAE business listing, Dubai Chamber member directory (if
    eligible), construction supplier directories.
  - Global: Crunchbase (free profile), Product Hunt (launch the calculator as a
    tool), AlternativeTo (list as a construction calculator).
- [ ] Create/complete social profiles with the same name, description and website
  link: LinkedIn Company Page, Facebook Page, YouTube channel, Instagram, X.
  Post the brick calculator once on each with a worked example (the 10×10 ft wall).
- [ ] Forums & communities (answer questions, link only where it genuinely helps):
  - Reddit: r/civilengineering, r/Construction, r/Pakistan (construction threads).
  - Quora: answer "how many bricks for a 5 marla house" and similar with the
    calculator link.
  - Facebook groups: Pakistan construction / house-construction groups, India
    home-construction groups, UAE villa construction groups.
  - Stack Exchange: Engineering (only where a calculation question is asked).
  - Never drop bare links; answer first, link as the source.

## Days 15–60 — Linkable assets (the pages that earn links by themselves)
- [ ] Publish the "Brick Rates Report — Pakistan, November 2026" as a dated page
  (PBS survey figures + kiln ranges, with the city table from the calculator).
  Update it monthly; dated rate reports are what Avenir's quarterly guides do,
  and they are the most-linked asset type among the competitors.
- [ ] Free PDF cheat sheet: "Brick Quantity Cheat Sheet — 9\" / 4.5\" / 13.5\" walls"
  (one page: bricks per sq ft, cement per CFT, sand per CFT, mix ratios). Offer it
  on the calculator page as a download; PDFs get linked from forums and WhatsApp
  groups.
- [ ] Embeddable widget: a small "Brick Calculator" iframe/embed snippet other
  sites can paste, with a "Powered by Civil Estimation Pro" link back to
  /brick-calculator. Offer it to construction blogs and material suppliers.
  (Build task — 1–2 days; the embed must include the backlink or it is pointless.)
- [ ] One YouTube video (screen recording, no face needed): "How many bricks for
  a 5 marla house? (calculated live)". Link the calculator in the description and
  pin a comment with the link. Same video, cut for LinkedIn and Facebook.

## Days 30–90 — Outreach (manual, no mass email)
- [ ] Guest posts (2–3): pitch a genuinely useful article to construction blogs
  in PK/IN/AE — e.g. "Brick rates in Pakistan: what the PBS survey actually says"
  for a Pakistani property blog; "IS 1077 brick sizes explained" for an Indian
  construction blog. One link to the calculator, in context, no demanded anchor.
- [ ] HARO-style: monitor HARO / Connectively / Qwoted for journalist requests
  about construction costs in Pakistan/India/UAE; answer as a quantity-surveying
  source with the rate report as evidence.
- [ ] Competitor-backlink replication: for each referring domain found in Week 0,
  decide if it is replicable (directory? forum? supplier list? blog?). Work the
  list top-down; skip anything paid, irrelevant or spammy.
- [ ] Supplier/kiln pages: ask 3–5 brick kilns and material suppliers you already
  work with to link the calculator from their "resources" or "rates" page (they
  get a useful tool for their customers; you get a relevant link).
- [ ] University/college resource pages: civil-engineering departments in Pakistan
  (UET Lahore, NUST, NED) and India (IITs, NITs) sometimes link free calculators
  from course resource pages. One polite email each, no follow-up spam.

## What NOT to do
- Do not buy links, directory "premium" listings, or PBN placements.
- Do not submit to auto-approve / spammy directories (they were excluded from the
  competitor audit for a reason — they do not help and can hurt).
- Do not copy competitor text, rates or tables into outreach; the rate report must
  cite its own sources (PBS survey + named market guides).
- Do not promise a ranking timeline to anyone. Report referring domains and
  Search Console impressions monthly instead.

## Monthly reporting (keep it simple)
- Referring domains (Search Console → Links, or the free checkers above).
- /brick-calculator impressions + clicks + average position (Search Console).
- Indexed programmatic URLs (site: search or Search Console coverage).
- PDF downloads + widget embeds (analytics events).
